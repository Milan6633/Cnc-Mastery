Complete Syllabus

20 modules across 4 levels — everything you need to go from beginner to professional CNC machinist. Curriculum compiled from NIMS, Haas, MIT OCW, DMG MORI, and top training programs.

Level 1

Foundation

Build the essential knowledge base

01

Shop Safety & Industrial Practices

Prerequisite for all other modules — covers OSHA standards, PPE, hazard communication, lockout/tagout procedures, and machine shop housekeeping.

2 lessons~8h
02

Shop Mathematics

Fractions, decimals, tolerances, trigonometry, feeds & speeds calculations, and coordinate geometry for machining.

3 lessons~12h
03

Blueprint Reading & Technical Drawing

Orthographic projections, dimensioning standards, tolerancing fundamentals, surface finish symbols, and reading real part prints.

12 lessons~10h
04

Precision Measurement & Metrology

Measuring instruments — calipers, micrometers, indicators, gauge blocks — plus calibration principles and CMM introduction.

13 lessons~10h
05

Materials Science for Machinists

Ferrous and non-ferrous metals, plastics, material properties (hardness, tensile strength, machinability), and heat treatment.

8 lessons~8h
Level 2

Core CNC Skills

Hands-on machining and G-code programming

06

Manual Machining Fundamentals

Engine lathe operations, vertical mill operations, drill press, surface grinding, and bench work fundamentals.

19 lessons~15h
07

Cutting Tools & Toolholders

Tool geometry, carbide insert selection (ISO/ANSI), end mills, drills, toolholder systems, speeds & feeds, and cutting fluids.

17 lessons~12h
08

Workholding Principles & Fixturing

3-2-1 locating principle, chucks, vises, collets, fixture plates, mandrels, and custom fixture design.

12 lessons~10h
09

CNC Machine Operation & Safety

CNC mill and lathe anatomy, control panels (Haas/Fanuc/Siemens), startup procedures, work coordinates, and tool offsets.

5 lessons~12h
10

G-Code Programming — Milling

Manual G-code for CNC milling: rapid/linear moves, arcs, canned cycles, cutter compensation, subprograms, and complete worked examples.

11 lessons~20h
11

G-Code Programming — Turning

Manual G-code for CNC turning: OD/ID operations, threading (G76), grooving, roughing/finishing cycles (G70/G71/G72), and worked examples.

9 lessons~18h
Level 3

Advanced CNC

CAD/CAM, GD&T, and quality systems

12

CNC Setup, Optimization & Troubleshooting

Job planning, setup sheets, first article inspection, cycle time optimization, chatter diagnosis, and common CNC alarms.

6 lessons~12h
13

CAD for Manufacturing

3D parametric modeling with Fusion 360 and SolidWorks — sketching, extrude, revolve, design for manufacturability (DFM).

18 lessons~15h
14

Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T)

ASME Y14.5 standard — datum systems, form/orientation/location/runout tolerances, MMC/LMC, and interpreting GD&T on part prints.

18 lessons~15h
15

CAM Programming — 2.5D & 3-Axis

CAM workflow from model to G-code — Fusion 360 and Mastercam toolpaths, simulation, verification, and post-processors.

20 lessons~18h
16

CAM Programming — Turning & Mill-Turn

Lathe CAM setup, OD/ID toolpath strategies, threading in CAM, live tooling, C-axis and Y-axis mill-turn operations.

13 lessons~14h
17

Quality Systems & Process Control

Quality management (ISO 9001), statistical process control (SPC), process capability (Cp/Cpk), inspection documentation, and AS9100.

12 lessons~10h
Level 4

Specialist Tracks

Multi-axis, automation, and Industry 4.0

18

Multi-Axis CNC Machining (4 & 5 Axis)

4th-axis rotary setup, 3+2 positional machining, simultaneous 5-axis concepts, CAM strategies, and multi-axis workholding.

15 lessons~15h
19

Advanced Programming & Automation

Macro B / parametric programming, probing cycles, bar feeders, pallet changers, and lights-out machining strategies.

5 lessons~15h
20

Industry 4.0 & Emerging Technologies

Digital twins, additive manufacturing, IoT monitoring, robotics integration, CNC career pathways, and building your portfolio.

1 lessons~10h
