About CNC Mastery

Why I Built This

I'm Milan Khadka — a mechanical engineer and CNC machinist trained at Varia (Vantaan ammattiopisto) in Finland. I've been through both the theoretical side of engineering and the practical, hands-on world of CNC machining.

When I was learning, I noticed something: the knowledge needed to become a skilled CNC machinist is scattered across expensive textbooks, locked behind school programs, and spread across dozens of YouTube channels with varying quality. There was no single, structured place to learn everything — from shop safety to 5-axis programming.

CNC Mastery is my answer to that problem. It's a complete, structured curriculum that covers everything a 2–3 year vocational program would teach, organized into clear modules and lessons that anyone can follow at their own pace.

The Curriculum

The syllabus isn't made up — it's compiled from real, respected training programs and certification standards:

  • NIMS (National Institute for Metalworking Skills) — Level I/II Machining Standards
  • Haas Automation — HTEC Curriculum & Certification Program
  • MIT OCW — 2.008 Design & Manufacturing II / 2.810 Manufacturing Processes
  • DMG MORI Academy — CNC training for Fachkraft certification
  • Autodesk Fusion 360 — CAD/CAM curriculum
  • America's Cutting Edge (ACE) — DoD-funded CNC & Metrology Training
  • Plus curricula from Central Piedmont CC, North Idaho College, Mesa Community College, Laney College, and more

Who This Is For

  • Complete beginners who want to enter the CNC machining field
  • Manual machinists transitioning to CNC
  • Engineering students who want practical machining knowledge
  • Hobbyists and makers with desktop CNC machines
  • Anyone preparing for NIMS or Haas CNC certifications
