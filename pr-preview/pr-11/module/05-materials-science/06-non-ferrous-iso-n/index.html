Non-Ferrous Metals (ISO N)

Classify non-ferrous materials in the ISO N group and describe their machining characteristics.

Learning objectives

  • State the hardness limit that defines the ISO N group.
  • List the main non-ferrous material families.
  • Describe chip control for aluminum.
  • Explain why maximum power must still be calculated.

What Are Non-Ferrous Materials?

ISO group N covers metals and non-metals that contain no iron.

Key concept

Softness is the defining trait here: everything in ISO N is iron-free and stays below roughly 130 HB.

The group includes:

  • Non-ferrous (Al) alloys with up to 22% silicon, which make up the largest part.
  • Copper, bronze, and brass.
  • Magnesium-based alloys.
  • Zinc-based alloys.
  • Plastics.
  • Composites such as Kevlar.

Machining Characteristics

  • Non-ferrous materials are long-chipping.
  • Chip control is relatively easy when the material is alloyed.
  • Aluminum is sticky and needs sharp cutting edges.
  • ISO N holds to a narrow band for both cutting force and the power a job needs.
Key concept

The specific cutting force kc1 for ISO N is 350–700 N/mm², the lowest of all six ISO groups.

Safety warning

Low cutting force is not the same thing as low power draw. Yes, non-ferrous materials take little power per mm³ — but push a high metal removal rate and the total can climb fast, so run the maximum-power calculation anyway.

High Speed, Sharp Edges

Non-ferrous materials reward high cutting speeds, but the soft, sticky behavior of aluminum places a firm demand on the tool.

Pro tip

Aluminum is sticky, so a sharp cutting edge and the correct geometry are essential to avoid built-up edge and poor surface finish.

Self-Check

1. What hardness limit defines the ISO N group?

Show Answer

Soft metals with a hardness under 130 HB.

2. Which material makes up the largest part of the ISO N group?

Show Answer

Non-ferrous aluminum alloys with up to 22% silicon.

3. What is the kc1 range for ISO N materials?

Show Answer

350–700 N/mm².

4. Why should you still calculate maximum power for ISO N materials?

Show Answer

Because the high metal removal rate means total power can still be significant, even though power per mm³ is low.
